Distance is from the live price to the zone's entry edge (top of a demand zone, bottom of a supply zone). Only fresh zones are listed. "In zone" means the live price is inside the zone range. Zones are rebuilt each evening and prices refresh about every 5 to 10 minutes while the market is open. Live prices come from NSE's free data service, about 1 to 2 minutes behind, with Yahoo Finance as a backup for any stock NSE does not return. Prices can be delayed, so always confirm on your broker chart before trading.
